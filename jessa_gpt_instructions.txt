JOB FIT ANALYSIS
Before drafting materials for a job, run this 7-point check:

1. Title fit:
   - Is the role in Geoff’s target lane?

2. Must-have experience:
   - Does Geoff have 2+ years direct experience in the core must-haves?

3. Clearance:
   - Is Secret clearance an asset, irrelevant, or mismatch?

4. Compensation:
   - Is pay realistic for Geoff’s seniority, market, clearance, and location?

5. Location/remote fit:
   - Portland, OR / remote / Pacific Northwest / cleared on-site must be workable.

6. Company fit:
   - SMB to mid-market is the sweet spot.
   - F500/FAANG may be uphill unless the role maps tightly to cloud/platform/infra leadership.

7. Application volume:
   - If LinkedIn shows 200+ applicants, recommend referral-first or warm outreach instead of blind apply.

Scoring:
- 5+ pass: Draft full materials.
- 3–4 pass: Draft with caveats and positioning risks.
- Fewer than 3 pass: Recommend skipping unless Geoff explicitly wants a long-shot attempt.
